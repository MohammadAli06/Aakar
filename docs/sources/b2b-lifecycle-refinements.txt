1. Problem: B2B order jumps too quickly
to bulk production

Our currently identified problem

Our previous flow was:

        Inquiry → Negotiation → Agreement → Order → Production

The weakness is that a large B2B buyer may commit to hundreds of products without any
intermediate validation.

Claude's contribution

Claude identified that the overall lifecycle needs failure/exception handling, but did not
consider a sample stage mandatory.

Gemini's contribution

Gemini specifically identified:
        Sample Order → Buyer Approval → Bulk Production

as a missing B2B procurement step.

Combined conclusion

We should not make sampling mandatory for every order.

Instead:

        CraftConnect supports an optional Sample Approval stage for
        high-volume/customized orders.

Final solution

Buyer Requirement

   ↓

Matching

   ↓

Inquiry

   ↓

Terms Discussion

   ↓

Sample Required?

↙         ↘
YES         NO

↓    ↓

Sample Bulk Order

↓

Buyer Approval

↓

Bulk Order

Example

Hotel wants 500 bamboo baskets with its logo.

Instead of immediately producing 500:

     Buyer requests 1 sample.

Artisan makes 1.

Buyer approves:

     “Shape, size and logo are correct.”

Then the 500-unit production order begins.

Why this matters: It reduces the risk of a large order being wrong before bulk production.

11. So THIS is our final problem set

Now, after combining both reviews, I would group everything into 6 actual problems instead
of presenting 15 tiny problems.

Problem 1 — Digitalization Barrier

Artisan has a physical product but struggles to create a professional digital B2B listing.
Solution

AI Product Digitization
Photo + Voice → AI → Catalogue → Verification → Pricing.

Problem 2 — B2B Discovery & Capability Mismatch

A buyer may need a specific quantity, deadline, customization and price, but ordinary
product discovery doesn't necessarily determine which artisan can actually fulfil it.

Solution

AI Requirement Understanding + Capability-Based Matching
Requirement → AI structures it → Match using:
Craft + Product + MOQ + Capacity + Lead Time + Price + Customization + Availability

Problem 3 — Communication & Negotiation Barrier

Buyer speaks in formal/commercial B2B language while artisan may prefer regional
language and voice.

Solution

AI Business Manager
Buyer requirement → simplified regional voice/text → artisan response → structured B2B
terms.

Problem 4 — B2B Transaction Risk

Bulk orders create risk around:
    ●​ samples
    ●​ payment before production
    ●​ production visibility
    ●​ quality issues
    ●​ disputes
Solution

Protected B2B Workflow

Sample*
↓
Structured Agreement
↓
Payment Terms
↓
Production
↓
Progress Check*
↓
Delivery
↓
Inspection
↓
Completion

* optional depending on order.

Problem 5 — Fulfilment Complexity

Artisan may struggle with packaging, shipping and tracking.

Solution

AI-Assisted Fulfilment

Packaging checklist → packaging verification → shipping reference → tracking → delivery.

CraftConnect coordinates the workflow, not the physical logistics infrastructure.

Problem 6 — Ecosystem / Marketplace Fragmentation

Artisan may become B2B-ready inside CraftConnect but still have different requirements for
external channels.

Solution

Dual Readiness + Integration Layer

Product

 ├── CraftConnect B2B Ready

        ├── Channel A
        ├── Channel B
        └── Government Channel

Actual automated external push remains future scope unless official integration access
exists.
