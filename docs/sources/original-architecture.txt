4. AI-GUIDED PHOTO CAPTURE

AI artisan ko photo lene mein guide karega.

Checks

    ●​ Lighting
    ●​ Framing
    ●​ Angle
    ●​ Product visibility
    ●​ Image quality
Example:

        “Photo thodi dark hai. Product ko light ke paas rakhiye.”

5. AI IMAGE ENHANCER & STUDIO

AI can:
    ●​ improve lighting
    ●​ remove/clean distracting background
    ●​ improve crop/framing
    ●​ enhance image quality

Authenticity rule

AI must preserve:
    ●​ real colour
    ●​ texture
    ●​ shape
    ●​ design
    ●​ craftsmanship
It should not artificially redesign the product.

6. VOICE-TO-CATALOG

Artisan simply speaks in their selected language.
Example:

        “Ye bamboo ka basket hai, maine haath se banaya hai…”
AI extracts relevant information such as:

    ●​ Product name
    ●​ Category
    ●​ Craft type
    ●​ Material
    ●​ Colour
    ●​ Size/dimensions
    ●​ Usage
    ●​ Craft details/story
    ●​ Other relevant attributes

7. ADAPTIVE AI QUESTIONS

No long form.
AI checks what it already knows.
If something is missing or uncertain:

        Only then ask.
Example:

        “Ek mahine mein kitne baskets bana sakte ho?”
If AI is uncertain about a field, it asks artisan to confirm instead of silently guessing.
8. AI CATALOG GENERATION

AI generates:

    ●​ Product title

    ●​ Craft-aware description
    ●​ Craft/story information

9. ARTISAN VOICE VERIFICATION

This is product-information verification, not KYC.

AI reads/speaks the generated information in artisan's language.

Example:

        “Maine samjha ki ye bamboo ka handwoven basket hai, 2 feet ka hai. Kya ye
        sahi hai?”

Artisan says:

        “Haan.”

or:

        “Nahi, size 1.5 feet hai.”

AI updates it.

AI Generated Information
      ↓

AI Reads It In Artisan's Language
      ↓

Artisan Confirms / Corrects
      ↓

Final Verified Product Information

34. BIDDING ACCESS CONTROL

No public unlimited bidding.

Only relevant/eligible buyers.

Possible criteria:

    ●​ Verified business
    ●​ Relevant category
    ●​ Relevant product interest
    ●​ Trust/reliability signals
    ●​ No serious risk flags
Participation can be limited.

36. SEALED BIDDING

Instead of a live price war:
Buyer submits:

    ●​ Quantity required
    ●​ Price offered
    ●​ Relevant terms
Then the session closes.

37. ARTISAN CONTROLS FINAL
SELECTION

Highest price does not automatically have to win.
Artisan can consider:
    ●​ Price
    ●​ Quantity
    ●​ Buyer reliability/trust
    ●​ Requirement fit

Artisan can:
    ●​ Select one buyer
    ●​ Select multiple buyers
    ●​ Split quantity
    ●​ Reject all

38. PRICE PROTECTION

Minimum acceptable price is controlled by artisan.
It can be informed by:
AI Pricing Assistant → suggested sustainable range → artisan decides threshold
No forced downward pricing.

39. BIDDING FAILURE CASES

No acceptable bids

Product returns to normal B2B availability.

Buyer backs out

Next suitable offer / normal B2B.

Artisan rejects all

Session closes without order.

Multiple buyers

Quantity may be split where appropriate.
